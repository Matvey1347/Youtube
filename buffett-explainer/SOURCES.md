# Narration fact check

- **First stock purchase:** Buffett and his sister purchased six shares of Cities Service preferred stock in 1942. The chronology is included in *Warren Buffett on Business* by Mary Buffett and David Clark: [O'Reilly book excerpt](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/warren-buffett-on/9780470570715/conn_9780470570715_oeb_ap1_r1.html).
- **First tax return and bicycle deduction:** Buffett wrote that he filed his first Form 1040 as a thirteen-year-old newspaper carrier and deducted $35 for a bicycle. See his [2003 Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2003ltr.pdf).
- **Long-term approach and compounding:** used as an explanatory summary of the investing principle, not as a numerical performance claim.
